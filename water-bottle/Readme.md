## Investigation Process

1. Read the challenge description carefully.
2. Identified **Boni Avenue** as the primary location.
3. Determined that Boni Avenue is located in **Mandaluyong City, Philippines**.
4. Searched for water refilling stations around the area using Google Maps.
5. Compared current establishments with historical Street View imagery.
6. Identified locations where different businesses existed in 2014.
7. Cross-referenced business names using public search engines.
8. Located a matching business record containing the required phone number prefix.
9. Combined the recovered business name and contact number to obtain the challenge flag.
